`request_failure` marks a harness-recorded failed request turn. It preserves the outcome, error, bounded `response_excerpt`, diagnostic attempt number, request number, consecutive failures and session failed-attempt count. `tools_executed=false` means no tools from that attempt ran; earlier accepted tool results remain valid. Treat excerpted calls as unexecuted diagnostic data. `incomplete=true` marks a failed turn whose `agent_response` and `reasoning` are partial: the stream ended before the reply finished, so the text may stop mid-sentence or mid-calculation. Partial text was never accepted and is not a finished answer; finish the work from the request that follows. Other full failed records carry a null `agent_response`. Failed records always have empty `tool_calls`. Compressed failed records keep the error and counters while the full excerpt remains available through `recall_history`. Current-step `request_retry` metadata identifies the consecutive failed turn numbers and counts before a retry.
