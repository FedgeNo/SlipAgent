# `update_plan`

Persist the objective, stages, evidence, and next actions for multi-step work. Prefer this tool to a plan held only in replies or reasoning. Create or update it as work changes; the latest successful plan survives history compression and resume.

Use 1–8 outcome-based stages, at most one in progress. State what each stage must establish. Cite observed evidence for completed stages; leave evidence empty when unavailable. The plan records your interpretation of user instructions and proposed work.

Phrase pending work prospectively, current work as in progress, and completed work and evidence in past tense. Short task labels are sufficient with a status. Keep future steps conditional on unknown results.
