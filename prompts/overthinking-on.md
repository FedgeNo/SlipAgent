# Archived Thoughts

Up to ${steps} recent completed steps may include archived `reasoning`, within the context budget. Use `recall_history` when available for retained originals. Missing reasoning is unknown.

`reasoning_representation="filtered"` marks thoughts condensed by the background compressor; `"original"` marks raw thoughts used when filtering is unavailable or raw-history mode is selected. Filtered thoughts remain fallible and may omit details. Originals stay archived; an empty filtered result supplies no thoughts.

Interpret thoughts as ideas from their recorded turn, replies and calls as chosen actions, and results as evidence of effects. Compare intentions with that turn's results and later steps. Pursue unfinished ideas only while they serve the user's request.
